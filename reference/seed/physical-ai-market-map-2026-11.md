# Physical AI Market Map (Nov 2026)

Nov 6, 2026 · @Josh

## How to read this

Companies are grouped by the customer they sell to, not by robot form factor. Funding and valuation figures move fast in this sector, and several come from reported talks rather than closed rounds; those are marked "reported". Where the best available source is a secondary aggregator rather than a press release or major outlet, treat the figure as directional.

Market context: Crunchbase counted $47.4B of venture funding into physical AI across 521 deals in H1 2026, close to 4x the $12B raised in H2 2025 ([Crunchbase News](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/physical-ai-funding-startups-robotics-aerospace-h1-2026/)).
